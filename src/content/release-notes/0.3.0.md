# hushtype 0.3.0

- **Cleaner transcripts.** Restarts and broken-off words are removed instead of being left in place or bridged with invented words.
- **A style for each mode.** Serious keeps full punctuation, Casual drops the closing period, and Very casual also lowercases sentence starts. Messaging starts as Casual. Styles never change words, so they also apply when cleanup is off or falls back to the raw transcript.
- **More time for long dictations.** The cleanup deadline grows with the length of what you said, up to four times the configured timeout.
- **Update checks.** hushtype looks for a newer version after launch and every six hours, then tells you with a notification and a menu bar entry. Updating opens Terminal, replaces the app, and asks for Accessibility again. Turn checks off in About if you prefer.
